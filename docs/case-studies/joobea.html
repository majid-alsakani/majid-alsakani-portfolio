01 · Executive summary

A job platform built around the real environment.

Joobea started from a local problem and became a full product system.

In Yemen, job opportunities were often distributed through closed chat groups, while CVs arrived as inconsistent files or images and employers had to screen applications manually. I built Joobea as a structured meeting point between talent and companies: job discovery, employer workflows, AI-assisted CV analysis, smart matching, freelance work, learning and mentorship.

The hard part was not adding a jobs table. It was making the whole flow useful in a market with variable connectivity, mobile-first usage, Arabic RTL requirements and data that was not structured for search. I owned the product direction, architecture, frontend, API, database, permissions, AI layer, delivery and roadmap.

02 · The problem

When opportunity is hard to discover.

The old workflow was fragmented.

Jobs disappeared inside fast-moving groups. CVs landed in inboxes without consistent structure. A company could have a real hiring need and a qualified person could have the right skills, but the two sides still missed each other because the information was difficult to find, compare and trust.

This was a systems problem.

The product needed to organize data, support different user roles, rank relevant opportunities, explain AI-assisted results and remain usable on phones and slower networks. The interface was only one part of the solution.

03 · Constraints as inputs

Designed for the conditions, not an idealized user.

Weak connectivityLightweight UI, progressive loading, optimized images and PWA-oriented delivery keep the core flow usable on constrained networks.
Arabic as a first-class languageRTL layout, typography and component behavior were considered from the beginning, with a complete English path.
Unstructured CVsResume analysis turns an uploaded document into an ATS-oriented score, strengths, gaps and useful next steps.
Multiple user rolesSeeker, employer and admin paths require different permissions, workflows and information boundaries.
More than full-time jobsFreelance opportunities, courses, mentors and recruitment fairs support different stages of a career.
Human decisions still matterMatching and scoring assist the workflow; they are not presented as an automatic hiring decision.
04 · The solution

A recruitment system, not a listing page.

The product is organized around the decisions each side needs to make.

For job seekers

Profiles, job discovery, filters, saved criteria, alerts, CV building and an ATS analysis loop that helps candidates improve before applying.

For employers

Company workspaces, job publishing, candidate pipelines, ranked applications and a clearer path from opening to shortlist.

For the market

Freelance work, courses, mentors and recruitment fairs broaden the platform beyond a single full-time hiring flow.

Smart matching

Skill, experience and location signals help rank relevant jobs or candidates without reducing a person to one score.

Arabic-first UX

Native RTL structure and bilingual content make the product feel designed for its users, not translated after the fact.

Production mindset

Dockerized delivery, CI checks, validation, pagination, caching and structured data support a system that can keep evolving.

05 · Architecture

Separate the product surface from the system decisions.

The architecture keeps user experiences, domain services, AI assistance and storage responsibilities distinct.

LayerResponsibilityKey design question
ClientReact, TypeScript, TailwindCSS, responsive web and PWA-oriented flowsCan the user understand the next action on a phone?
API boundaryFastAPI REST endpoints, typed models, validation, rate limiting and RBACCan every request be trusted, scoped and evolved?
Domain servicesJobs, profiles, applications, companies, alerts, marketplace and analyticsWhich business rule belongs to which service?
AI layerResume parsing, ATS scoring, matching and re-rankingHow can assistance remain useful and reviewable?
DataPostgreSQL for structured data, object storage for files and a cache layerWhat should be searchable, private, temporary or durable?
06 · AI assistance

Make the model useful inside a workflow.

The AI layer is designed around an actual user decision. A CV is analyzed for ATS-oriented signals, strengths and missing skills. Matching then uses available profile and job signals to rank relevant opportunities. The output helps a candidate improve a profile or helps an employer start a review from a more relevant shortlist.

The boundary matters: an AI score is not a final hiring decision. The system should make the recommendation understandable enough to review, and future iterations should measure quality by job type, language and experience level rather than treating one aggregate score as the whole truth.

The product question was not “How do I add AI?” It was “Where can assistance reduce repetitive work while keeping the human decision visible?”

09 · Results and measurement

Build the measurement plan with the product.

Joobea is live at joobea.com with real product surfaces for candidates and companies. The current evidence is qualitative: the platform brings structured profiles, job discovery, employer workflows, AI-assisted resume feedback and matching into one Arabic-first experience.

The next measurement pass should replace broad claims with a baseline and a method. I would track time to shortlist, quality of matching through blinded human review, CV extraction success, Core Web Vitals on target devices, application completion and the effect of ATS feedback on profile quality. I would publish only results that can be reproduced.

Do not invent metricsNumbers such as match quality, users or time saved need a source and a clear calculation.
Measure the workflowThe strongest outcome is not a feature count; it is a better path from need to useful action.
10 · Lessons

What the build changed in my thinking.

Context is architecture.

Weak connectivity, phones and RTL are not polish items. They change how the product should load, communicate and structure information.

AI needs a decision boundary.

A recommendation becomes valuable when the user understands what it means, what it does not mean and what to do next.

Joobea taught me that a production product is not a collection of screens. It is a set of decisions connected by data, permissions, feedback and operational constraints.
11 · Next

The roadmap beyond the first release.

The product roadmap includes broader geographic coverage, more companies and organizations, a university-to-employment gateway, mobile applications, training tracks connected to real opportunities, a public partner API, deeper semantic search, employer screening assistance, interview scheduling and market-salary intelligence.

The engineering priority is to grow the system without losing the qualities that made it useful: a clear workflow, careful permissions, explainable assistance, Arabic-first interaction and performance that respects the user’s environment.
