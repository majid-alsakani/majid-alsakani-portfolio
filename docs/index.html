How I work

Engineering with a point of view.

The best systems are not the most complicated ones. They are the ones that make a difficult thing feel clear.

From Yemen, for the real world.

I design and ship end-to-end systems with Python, FastAPI, Django, React and TypeScript. My work spans AI-powered job matching, learning products, developer tools and the infrastructure that makes them dependable.

Useful over impressiveTechnology should solve a real problem before it tries to look clever.
Built for constraintsLow bandwidth, mobile devices and imperfect conditions are design inputs.
Clarity is a featureEvery interface and API should help people understand what happens next.
Ship and learnSmall releases, observable systems and honest feedback beat big assumptions.
