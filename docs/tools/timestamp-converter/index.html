Time utility / 03

Time, made
legible.

BROWSER-LOCALThe conversion happens in this browser. Nothing you enter is submitted to a server.
Numbers with 10 digits are treated as seconds; 13 digits are milliseconds.
Your local and UTC time will appear here.

Use it well

Keep the clock.
Keep the context.

Enter a Unix value in seconds or milliseconds, or an ISO date such as 2026-08-28T12:00:00Z. The converter shows the same moment in the browser’s local locale, as UTC, and as both common Unix representations.

Use ISO dates with an explicit time zone when a timestamp needs to mean the same thing across services. A bare date may be interpreted differently by a browser and a backend service.

How does it identify seconds or milliseconds?

Numeric values below 100 billion are interpreted as seconds; larger values are interpreted as milliseconds. You can always verify the converted ISO result.

Why is local time different for teammates?

Local time reflects each browser’s time zone. The UTC and Unix values identify the same instant independently of that time zone.

Does the tool store the timestamp?

No. Conversion happens locally in the browser and the value is not submitted by this page.
