# Cinematic asset audit

The repository includes a 1920×640 Joobea banner that combines product identity, dashboard context, and product capabilities. It is suitable for a wide full-bleed reveal and horizontal camera pan, but not for a small card.

The repository also contains a detailed Multi-Agent architecture graphic. Its current high-contrast magenta treatment is unsuitable as a direct full-page background, but a carefully cropped, tinted and masked version can be used as a short “systems under the surface” moment behind the Omni sequence.

The strongest scene palette is therefore: Joobea as wide signal/product opening, Sini as personal progress interface, and Omni as an inspectable system trace. The page should animate these real proofs with camera-like crops, depth, and pinned scroll choreography.

## Visual verification

The updated local desktop preview now displays a high-contrast Signal Noir introduction with a diamond-masked portrait, expanded visual field, signal markers and a dramatic light hierarchy. The transition into the white editorial interlude creates a purposeful contrast before the Joobea product reel, which uses the wide original product banner as a full scene rather than an interchangeable project card.

Within the pinned reel, the first Joobea scene is active at the initial scroll position. Its labelled system interface, cinematic crop, scan grid, HUD and supporting copy confirm that product evidence is now the main visual subject rather than decoration.

The reel transition was tested at the second stage: the active scene changed to Sini Alkhafif and the HUD changed to `02`. The full viewport then displayed the Sini interface, local scene marker, progress-led headline and its associated constraint and decision. This confirms that the chapter responds to scroll position rather than presenting three static scenes.
