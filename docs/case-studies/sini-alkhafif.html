Sini Alkhafif — Case Study

By Majid Al-Sakani (ماجد السكني) · Full Stack Developer, Yemen · Updated 2026-08-03

PythonDjangoPostgreSQLJavaScriptDocker

The Problem

Learners had scattered material with no sense of progress: no structured path, no retention loop and no feedback on what was already mastered.

The Solution

A lesson engine with structured units, a personal dashboard, progress persistence and a clean distraction-free reading mode.

Results

DimensionOutcome
StructureContent organized into ordered units with clear completion state
RetentionProgress tracking gives learners a visible reason to return
PerformanceLightweight pages load quickly on constrained networks
ExperienceArabic-first interface designed for readability

My Role

I designed the architecture, implemented the backend and frontend, set up CI/CD and Docker delivery, and own the ongoing roadmap of the product.
